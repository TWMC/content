Description: 

During this winter season, "stardust cruising ropeway" is available.
https://www.mt-tsukuba.com/?p=1723

After sunset, we will start hiking from the Tsutsujigaoka (つつじヶ丘) ropeway station and climb to the Nyotai san summit (female peak, 女体山).
Then, we will enjoy the night view from the mountain and take a ropeway to go back to the car parking lot.
Head/hand lamp and warm clothes are necessary.
A restaurant is scheduled to be open until the last ropeway.

Please note that depending on weather condition, the ropeway operation can be canceled.
Indeed, we needed to walk down the mountain because of strong wind.
When there is no ropeway customer, the ropeway operation can be terminated.

Difficulty level : 2 out of 5.
Meting time and place : 16:00 at Tsukuba center car parking
