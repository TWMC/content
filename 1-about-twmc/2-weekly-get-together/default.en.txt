Title: Get Together

----

Text: 

TWMC has a tradition of getting together for our monthly meetings.
In the past, we used to meet at Hot Staff. We are now holding our monthly meetings at Engi Cafe.

Our monthly meeting is held on the** 1st Wednesday of each month **from 8:00 pm.

Engi Cafe is located about a 5-minute walk from Tsukuba Station in Tsukuba, Ibaraki.
<https://beercafe-engi.studio.site>

If you want to be sure to find someone, please contact one of the committee members.
